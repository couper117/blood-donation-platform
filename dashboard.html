    Platform overview

    Awaiting approval

    New hospitals and pharmacies cannot subscribe, post blood requests or sell medicines until you approve them. Approval starts their free 7-day trial. Check they are a real, licensed organisation first.

    Medical documents review

    Screening certificates and prescriptions, with the automatic AI plausibility check. Flagged documents (duplicates, wrong document type, unreadable) appear first - your decision is final, the AI only flags.

    All accounts

    Approved organisations and registered donors. "Reset password" creates a temporary password to give the account holder privately.

    Feedback

    Audit log

    A record of recent administrator actions (approvals, password resets, document reviews, moderation).
