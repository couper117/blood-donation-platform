Why donate

  • One donation can help up to three patients.
  • Blood cannot be manufactured - it only comes from donors.
  • Most healthy adults can donate every 56 days.
  • Your details stay private and secure.

Want a full donor account instead?

A donor account is separate from a donation offer. With an account you get a profile with your donation history, hospital verification of your screening certificate, a Verified badge, your own dashboard, and the ability to book blood requests.
