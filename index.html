About this platform

Rwanda is a world leader in delivering blood by drone to hospitals in hard-to-reach areas. This platform brings that idea together with everyday donor management: it keeps donor information confidential, shows where blood is needed most, and helps the right unit of blood get to the right patient quickly - whether by road or by air. This is a demonstration project built for educational purposes.

Rwanda Blood Donation Centre - demonstration project. Not a real medical service.
