Subscription plans

Hospitals and pharmacies pay a monthly subscription to use this platform - hospitals to post blood requests and use drone delivery, pharmacies to sell medicines. Payment is real, processed securely by Flutterwave (MTN/Airtel Mobile Money or card), and your plan activates only after the payment is verified.
