Medicines

Only medicines for the health cases this platform covers - anemia & blood support, diabetes, hypertension, asthma, heart health and obesity - sold by registered pharmacies. Medicines marked Prescription needed require you to upload your doctor's prescription before buying. Pay securely online (Mobile Money / card) or reserve and pay at the pharmacy.

This information is general and educational only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice - always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
